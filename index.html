01 / DAILY TRACKING

Track all five daily prayers.

Log Fajr, Dhuhr (Jummah on Fridays), Asr, Maghrib, and Isha with a tap. Track optional Tahajjud separately. Mark prayers On Time, Qaza, Missed, or Excused, and revisit any day in the calendar.

02 / TIMES & REMINDERS

Stay close to your rhythm.

View prayer times by city and country, with cached times available offline. Enable an evening reminder to finish your log. Premium adds location settings and individual alerts at prayer time or up to an hour before.

03 / STATISTICS & TRENDS

See the bigger picture.

Review today, this week, this month, and the year. Compare On Time, Qaza, Missed, Excused, and Not Added records through seven-day charts, monthly comparisons, and yearly summaries.

04 / STREAKS & MILESTONES

Consistency with compassion.

Follow current and best streaks, perfect days, and milestones. Excused prayers during menstruation or postnatal recovery protect streaks and completion rates. Premium adds advanced analysis and a 30-day heatmap.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A simpler start.

Can I use Salah Tracker App without an account?

Yes. Guest mode stores records locally. Sign in with Google when you want to back up and sync across Android devices.

How are excused prayers handled?

Female users can mark prayers as Excused during menstruation or postnatal recovery. These entries do not unfairly break streaks or reduce completion rates.

Does the app work offline?

You can continue editing your prayer log offline, and cached prayer times remain available. Pending sync changes retry when connectivity returns.

Is the free plan ad-free too?

Yes. Free and Premium are both ad-free. Core tracking, statistics, daily reminders, the widget, guest mode, and cloud sync remain free.
